Booking Systems

A WhatsApp booking button is not the same as a calendar and appointment booking system.

Some businesses only need a strong WhatsApp booking route. Others need proper appointment handling with availability, calendars, confirmations, reminders and internal admin views. The page now makes that difference much clearer so clients understand what they are actually buying.

Simple Booking Route

WhatsApp buttons, enquiry forms and lead capture

  • Best when you want people to message you directly.
  • Good for manual booking, consultations or warm lead handling.
  • Can include landing pages, enquiry forms and WhatsApp-first follow-up.
  • Does not include live calendar availability or appointment management logic.

Appointment System

Calendar-based booking logic and appointment workflows

  • Best for clinics and businesses that need actual appointment handling.
  • Can include calendars, availability, confirmations, reminders and rescheduling.
  • Can also include admin dashboards, appointment status views and practitioner workflows.
  • This is a deeper system build, not just a button that sends people to WhatsApp.

What custom booking logic can include

Appointment pages, consultation requests, calendar rules, booking confirmations, reminders, AWS SES email notifications, rescheduling, payments, dashboards, status tracking and operational workflows that support how the business actually runs day to day.
