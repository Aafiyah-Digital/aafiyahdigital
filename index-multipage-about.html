About

Built by a full-stack developer, marketer and automation builder who understands how websites and systems need to work together.

Aafiyah Digital was created to help businesses stop relying on disconnected tools and start using websites, booking flows and client journey systems that are clearer, faster and easier to grow with.

Founder-Led Expertise

Aafiyah Digital is led by a full-stack developer, marketer and automation builder with over a decade of hands-on experience across e-commerce, digital products, consultations, email marketing and social platforms. The work is also backed by AWS Certified full-stack expertise, bringing together both strategic thinking and strong technical foundations.

Why This Work Matters

The experience behind Aafiyah Digital includes building and running SunnaHealing UK for over 13 years, transitioning it from a successful physical e-commerce business into a digital-first brand powered by content, copywriting, funnels, email campaigns and community.

Strategy Plus Technical Depth

Along the way, that work evolved into deep experience in conversion-focused copywriting, funnel design, email marketing and digital strategy, alongside the technical delivery of payments, automations and infrastructure.

Why Aafiyah Digital Exists

After seeing firsthand the impact of SaaS overload, rising monthly costs, platform limits and the constant pressure of replying to comments, DMs and inboxes, Aafiyah Digital was created to build ethical, privacy-first systems that businesses actually own, designed to save time, reduce dependency on tools and scale sustainably.

Ethical

Systems should support better communication and better journeys, not push manipulative tactics.

Privacy-first

Infrastructure choices and automation logic should respect user data and reduce unnecessary exposure.

Ownership

Clients should have a system that grows with them instead of trapping them in rented tools.

Let’s build a cleaner route for your business

If your leads are getting stuck between social media, WhatsApp, your website and too many disconnected tools, Aafiyah Digital can help you create a much clearer system.
