Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 December 2025

Aafiyah Digital is committed to ethical, privacy-first automation. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our website, services, and automation systems.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, and details you choose to submit
  • Messages sent via contact forms or connected platforms
  • Automation events (comments, messages, webhook payloads)
  • Minimal server logs for security and reliability

2. How Your Information Is Used

  • Deliver requested automations and services
  • Send resources or emails you explicitly opt into
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests
  • Maintain system security and performance

We do not sell, rent, trade, or exploit personal data.

3. Platform Integrations

Our systems may integrate with Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, email delivery providers, and payment platforms. Data received from these platforms is used solely for the intended automation purpose and handled in accordance with platform policies.

4. Facebook & Instagram Data Use

Aafiyah Digital Automation integrates with the Facebook Graph API and Instagram Graph API to automate responses to Page comments, post interactions, and direct messages.

  • We may receive public comment data (comment text and comment ID)
  • We may receive Messenger or Instagram Direct messages sent to a connected Page or account
  • We may receive the public profile ID (PSID) required to send automated replies

This data is used exclusively to deliver automated responses requested by the Page owner. We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising, profiling, or third-party marketing.

No unnecessary personal data is stored. Where message or comment data is processed, it is retained only as required for automation delivery and system reliability.

5. Data Ownership

Clients retain ownership of their data at all times. Our systems are designed to minimise data retention, avoid unnecessary tracking, and reduce platform lock-in.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies and analytics strictly for performance and reliability. No invasive tracking, profiling, or data brokering is used.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, or loss.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
