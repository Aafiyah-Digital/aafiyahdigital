Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 April 2026

Aafiyah Digital is committed to ethical, privacy-first automation. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our website, services, and automation systems.

This includes website enquiries, client communications, CRM and automation workflows, and Meta app features used for Instagram and Facebook comment-to-DM workflows, webhook-triggered automations, and related messaging systems requested by connected page or account owners.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, and details you choose to submit
  • Messages sent via contact forms, email, WhatsApp, or connected platforms
  • Automation events, including comments, messages, webhook payloads, and booking-flow triggers
  • Minimal server, delivery, and security logs for reliability and abuse prevention

2. How Your Information Is Used

  • Deliver requested automations and services
  • Send resources or emails you explicitly opt into
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests
  • Maintain system security and performance

We do not sell, rent, trade, or exploit personal data.

3. Platform Integrations

Our systems may integrate with Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, email delivery providers, payment platforms, and customer-facing booking experiences. Aafiyah Digital operates the automation system, and some client journeys may direct users to booking experiences hosted on app.hijama.bio.

Data received from these platforms is used solely for the intended service or automation purpose and handled in accordance with applicable platform policies.

4. Facebook & Instagram Data Use

Aafiyah Digital Automation integrates with the Facebook Graph API and Instagram Graph API to automate responses to Page comments, post interactions, and direct messages for connected businesses.

  • We may receive public comment data, including comment text and comment ID
  • We may receive Messenger or Instagram Direct messages sent to a connected Page or account
  • We may receive platform-scoped account identifiers required to send automated replies
  • We may receive webhook event payload data needed to trigger a comment-to-DM workflow
  • We may receive delivery, troubleshooting, and security logs needed to confirm message handling and prevent abuse

This data is processed only to detect trigger comments, send the configured reply or direct message, deliver a requested booking link, troubleshoot failures, and protect the system from abuse. In some cases, data may be processed in connection with linked booking journeys, including when a private message contains a booking link for a client service flow hosted on app.hijama.bio.

Webhook, comment, message, and related delivery log data is retained only for as long as necessary to deliver the requested automation, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, and comply with legal obligations.

Aafiyah Digital processes this data on behalf of the business using the automation. The purpose is limited to replying, routing, and fulfilling the requested workflow, and Meta-derived data is not used for advertising, profiling, audience building, resale, or unrelated marketing.

5. Data Ownership

Clients retain ownership of their data at all times. Our systems are designed to minimise data retention, avoid unnecessary tracking, and reduce platform lock-in.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies and analytics strictly for performance and reliability. No invasive tracking, profiling, or data brokering is used.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, or loss.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

9. Data Deletion Requests

If you wish to request deletion of data associated with your Facebook or Instagram interaction, please email:

hello@aafiyahdigital.com

We begin processing once the request contains enough information to identify the relevant interaction.

You can also review our full deletion process on the Data Deletion page.
