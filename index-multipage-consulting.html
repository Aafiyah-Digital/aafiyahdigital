Consulting

Digital and social media consulting for brands that need clearer strategy, stronger positioning and better conversion.

Aafiyah Digital provides digital consulting, social media consulting, digital audits and social media audits for brands that need expert review, practical direction and a more intentional route from visibility to enquiries, bookings and sales.

Digital audits

Review the full customer journey across website, social traffic, booking flow and follow-up to find friction, missed opportunities and weak conversion points.

Outcome: clearer priorities and more informed next steps.

Social media consulting

Audit Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp touchpoints to improve offer clarity, CTA strength, lead routing and how social attention turns into action.

Outcome: stronger messaging and better lead handling from social traffic.

Sales and marketing support

Get practical recommendations on positioning, conversion friction, funnel structure, page strategy and what to improve first for better results.

Outcome: more focused marketing decisions with less guesswork.

What This Can Cover

Digital audits and social media consulting built around real brand and conversion problems.

The work can focus on one area or review the wider digital setup. This includes social media presence, website clarity, enquiry flow, booking steps, offer positioning, content direction, social media audit findings and where leads are dropping off between interest and action.

The goal is to identify what is slowing growth, what is confusing potential clients and what needs to be improved first, whether that leads into consulting only or a later build.

Who It Is For

Brands that know something needs improving but need clarity before building.

This is especially useful for service businesses, consultants, clinics, wellness brands, appointment-led businesses and founders who are already getting some attention but want stronger conversion, better messaging and a more professional digital setup.

Why It Matters

Better decisions before more spend.

A good audit or consulting review can stop brands wasting time and money on the wrong page, the wrong platform, the wrong offer structure or the wrong tool stack. It creates a clearer route to more meaningful improvements.

Consulting can be booked on its own or paired with a build.

Some clients need strategic review only. Others use consulting as the first step before a website, landing page, booking system, branded biolink or social client journey project. Either way, the focus stays on clarity, conversion and building the right next step.

Digital consulting FAQ

What does digital and social media consulting include?

It can include digital audits, social media audits, messaging and positioning review, bio and CTA review, website conversion feedback, booking flow review, sales journey review and practical recommendations to improve how your brand turns attention into enquiries and sales.

Who is this consulting best suited to?

It is especially useful for service businesses, consultants, clinics, wellness brands and appointment-based businesses that already get some attention online but need clearer strategy and stronger conversion before investing in a larger build.

Can I book consulting without booking a website project?

Yes. Consulting can be booked as a standalone service or used as the first step before a website, landing page, booking system, branded biolink or social client journey build.
