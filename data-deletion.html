Data Deletion Instructions

Aafiyah Digital respects your privacy and your right to control your data. This page explains how you can request the deletion of any personal data that may be processed through our systems.

What data may be processed

Depending on the automation in use, we may temporarily process limited data such as: social media usernames, comments, messages, or email addresses submitted voluntarily through forms or automations. We do not sell, rent, or exploit personal data.

How to request data deletion

To request deletion of your data, please email:

hello@aafiyahdigital.com

Please include:

  • Your name or username
  • The platform involved (e.g. Instagram, Facebook)
  • A brief description of the data you want deleted

Processing timeframe

Data deletion requests are processed within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 7–30 days, unless legal or security obligations require temporary retention.

Automation & third-party platforms

Some automations rely on third-party platforms such as Meta (Facebook, Instagram), payment processors, or email providers. Where applicable, users may also manage or delete their data directly through those platforms.
